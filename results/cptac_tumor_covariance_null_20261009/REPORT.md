## What was tested

Use the committed CPTAC PDC000127 proteomics matrix and labels; apply the original `confound_audit_cptac_ccrcc.preprocess` to the *mixed* cohort (zero-imputation, per-sample median-centering, top-2,000 most variable proteins, and feature standardization), then restrict to **110 tumor samples**. This fixes the original mixed-cohort feature selection and does not create an independently selected tumor-specific panel.

For the tumor-only sample-by-protein matrix, subtract tumor feature means and compute an exact singular-value decomposition. The observed statistic is the maximum finite one-dimensional (H1) persistence of the tumor-only **PCA(50) Euclidean score cloud**, using `ripser`.

Under the conditional null, Haar-uniform random rotations of the *sample-centered row subspace* preserve **the full tumor-only feature covariance matrix, means, singular-value spectrum and PCA variance** exactly (up to floating-point error). They alter the relative placement of individual tumor sample points. This is **not** the prior independent-column permutation null and does **not** preserve each protein's empirical marginal distribution. The covariance statement is an exact algebraic invariant, checked by synthetic unit tests; it should not be confused with evidence that real patients were generated by the row-exchangeable Gaussian model.

The 499-draw run used the predeclared **independent seed 20261010**. The 24-draw pilot used seed 20261009, and its results were not pooled into the primary Monte Carlo reference.

**Result:** The observation does **not** exceed the covariance-preserving null at the prospectively declared one-sided 0.05 threshold. The tail-probability interval quantifies **Monte Carlo draw uncertainty conditional on the reference model**; it is not a confidence interval for biological effect sizes or patient-level sampling.

This is a **non-rejection**, not a demonstration of no biological topology. Observed loops could come from covariance, higher-order differences not distinguishable under these data/assumptions, patient- or batch-dependence, or other effects.

## What this does and does not contradict

The earlier [CPTAC class-conditioned feature-permutation sensitivity](../cptac_resid_null_sensitivity_20261009/REPORT.md) obtained `p=0.002` for a **different** statistic: class-mean residualization on a **mixed 194-sample tumor/normal** population followed by PCA(50), compared against an independent-within-class column-shuffle null. The new tumor-only covariance-preserving analysis has changed **both the cohort subset and the null construction**. The difference between those p-values does **not** identify covariance as the cause of the earlier result; presenting it as such would confound the comparison.

This experiment asks one narrower question: how unusual is the tumor-only, covariance-fixed PCA(50) H1 statistic under a conditional sample-space Haar reference? It does **not** establish that the original 194-sample result disappears under a covariance-preserving, label-conditioned control. The next causal-isolation step would compare null families on the **identical fixed tumor-only input, PCA score definition, sample size, and topological statistic**, with both controls fully specified before interpreting their difference.

## Assumptions still open

This reference is conditionally calibrated only if row orientations are exchangeable under the chosen Gaussian/matrix-normal model **for the fixed preprocessed and selected tumor-only matrix**. The code does not establish tumor sample independence, patient identity, batch effects, tissue heterogeneity, non-Gaussian measurement noise, or representativeness of the source cohort. Rotations keep covariance but not individual feature marginals, so a non-rejection is not evidence that every biologically meaningful alternative is covered. No held-out tumor/normal prediction claim is tested here.

The [methodological correction to the original manuscript](../../paper/METHODOLOGICAL_CORRECTIONS_2026-10-09.md) is independent of this outcome and remains in force regardless of the reported p-value.
